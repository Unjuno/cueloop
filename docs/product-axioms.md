# Product Axioms

## Purpose

CUELOOP helps a person think without replacing the person's reasoning.

## Axioms

1. **Human-first generation**  
   The user attempts to generate, explain, predict, or decide before the system supplies content.

2. **Silence is an action**  
   No intervention is often preferable to a late, redundant, or disruptive intervention.

3. **Minimal intervention**  
   The system returns the smallest intervention expected to improve the user's next cognitive action.

4. **Separation of concerns**  
   Knowledge input, generation elicitation, verification, and consolidation are separate modules.

5. **No default answer completion**  
   Completed answers are an explicit fallback, not the default behavior.

6. **Deterministic delivery control**  
   Language models may propose state hypotheses and intervention candidates. A deterministic controller decides whether an intervention is delivered.

7. **Operational states, not diagnoses**  
   Human-state labels describe interaction conditions and must not be treated as stable judgments of personality, intelligence, or mental health.

8. **Assistance should fade**  
   Repeatedly successful interventions should become shorter, less frequent, or disappear.

9. **Verification must preserve agency**  
   The verifier identifies unsupported claims, logical gaps, and scope errors without rewriting the user's reasoning by default.

10. **No unsupported enhancement claims**  
    The project must not claim general cognitive improvement without adequate evidence.

## Initial scope

The first target is a single-user, voice-first session lasting 10–30 minutes for:

- research hypothesis development
- product and system design
- complex decision-making
- argument development
- technical problem decomposition

## Explicit non-goals

- medical or psychological diagnosis
- therapy or clinical treatment
- generalized intelligence scoring
- autonomous behavior modification
- continuous interruption
- hidden recording
- implicit collection of sensitive voice data
